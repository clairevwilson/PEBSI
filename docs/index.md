# PEBSI

**Python Energy Balance model for Snow and Ice**

PEBSI is an open-source glacier energy balance model that simulates the surface energy balance and subsurface mass balance at the point scale. It is coupled to a neural network emulator of the Snow, Ice and Aerosol Radiative transfer model [SNICAR](https://github.com/jmcook1186/biosnicar-py) which simulates snow and firn albedo.
