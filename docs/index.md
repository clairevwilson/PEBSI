# PEBSI

**Python Energy Balance model for Snow and Ice**

PEBSI is an open-source glacier energy balance model that simulates the surface energy balance and subsurface mass balance at the point scale. It is coupled to the Snow, Ice and Aerosol Radiative transfer model [SNICAR](https://github.com/nruth/snicar_driver) which simulates snow and firn albedo.
